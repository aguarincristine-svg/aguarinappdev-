## Why I Love Naruto 
- The story is inspiring and emotional.

- Every character has their own struggles and dreams.

1. **Naruto Uzumaki** - A determined ninja who dreams of becoming Hokage.
2. **Sasuke Uchiha** - A talented ninja seeking strength and revenge.
3. **Sakura Haruno** - A skilled medical ninja and member of Team 7.
4. **Kakashi Hatake** - The wise and powerful leader of Team 7.
5. **Hinata Hyuga** - A kind and brave ninja who admires Naruto.
